Choose your outlaw duck

Donald has classic white Pekin plumage and an orange bill. Daisy has Cayuga black-green iridescent feathers. Dally has Saxony silver-buff and chestnut markings.

Donald: balanced · Daisy: faster · Dally: stronger shot and higher jump.

Outlaw armory & upgrades

The starter sidearm is the Jesse James Colt .44. Later gear is western-inspired and named for famous outlaws; the game uses cartoon, non-realistic action.
